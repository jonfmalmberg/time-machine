# nurthi: notes

## (a) Confidence

**`nurthi`.** I am fairly sure of the definition and much less sure of the playlist's breadth and of the size. All 84 claims are `found`. The definition rests on English Wikipedia (Music of Sri Lanka, Sri Lanka, Theatre of Sri Lanka, John de Silva), Sinhala Wikipedia (Tower Hall Theatre Foundation, the gramophone era, Danno Budunge) and three newspaper essays drawing on Sarachchandra's *The Folk Drama of Ceylon* (Sunday Times 2016, Daily FT 2016 and 2020, thuppahis.com). They agree on the Parsi-theatre origin (Elphinstone Dramatic Company, 1880s), North Indian melodies, C. Don Bastian then John de Silva, and the decline with cinema. The start date differs: the Sunday Times gives Bastian's *Rolina* in 1879; the tree's 1886 is English Wikipedia's Ramayanaya.

**The playlist is narrow.** 21 of 24 songs come from one source: the Tower Hall Theatre Foundation's *Tower Hall Nurthi Gee* (16 volumes, ℗ 2023 Tower Studio). Their tie is the album title, which names the genre. The singers and recording dates are not given, and which play each track comes from is inferred from its title. "Siri Sanga Bodhi" also has a page tie: FT 2020 lists it among songs from John de Silva productions. The other three songs are later renditions by named singers:
- Rukmani Devi's and W. D. Amaradeva's "Danno Budunge". Sinhala Wikipedia places the song in de Silva's nurthi *Siri Sangabo*, and FT names both renditions.
- Wasantha Sandanayaka's "Lona Muni Rajage" (2000). It is tied **by title only** to the song that English and Sinhala Wikipedia call a nurti song. It is a modern devotional-style band version.

All Tower Hall years are the 2023 digital release, not recording years.

## (c) Neighbours with no id in the tree
- **Nadagam.** This is the Sinhala folk-opera of the coast that nurthi replaced, a natural near-miss.
- **Kolam and sokari.** These are the older masked and folk dramas.
- **Parsi theatre music.** This is the Hindustani/Urdu/Gujarati stage song nurthi copied, as in *Indrasabha* and Baliwalla's company. It is not `hindustani` classical.
- **Sinhala Buddhist devotional song (bodu gee / bhakti gee).** Later renditions of "Danno Budunge" and "Lona Muni Rajage" sit on bodu-gee albums.
- **Minerva-troupe stage comedy (B. A. W. and Eddie Jayamanne).** This was the stage bridge into Sinhala film.

Both near-misses go to `sinhalafilmsong`, which exists: Asokamala and Kadawunu Poronduwa, both 1947.

## (d) What fell short
- **No early recordings.** No recording of the original stage era (1880s–1920s) was found on a readable page. The first nurthi 78s, the 1908 P. Orr record and Radio Ceylon transcriptions all have no catalogue record.
- **The Tower Hall series** gives no singers or recording years.
- **Radio-grade nurthi singers** (Shrilal Abeykoon, Ramya Wanigasekara, Manike Attanayake, Abeywardena Balasuriya) have nothing on iTunes LK, and MusicBrainz has no nurthi releases.
- **Gerald Wickremesooriya's Nurthi Gee record** was not found.
- **Sinhala script** comes only for the two songs whose Sinhala titles appear on Sinhala Wikipedia. The Tower Hall titles stay in iTunes' romanisation.
- **Sunday Observer 2011 article:** it answered with a bot check and was not used.
- **English Wikipedia's "1903 album via Radio Ceylon"** contradicts its own date for Radio Ceylon (1925), so it is used only as a size pointer.
- **The size** is not based on any catalogue count.
