# gambangkromong: notes

## (a) Confidence

**`gambangkromong`.** I am fairly sure of the songs and the definition, and unsure of the size and of many years. All 70 claims are `found`. The tracks were read from the raw iTunes lookup JSON and the MusicBrainz web service. Each song is tied to the genre in one of three ways:
- **The album, EP or group name contains "gambang kromong".** This is the main tie for 24 of 29 songs: the Smithsonian Folkways *Music of Indonesia, Vol. 3 … Gambang Kromong*, *100% Gambang Kromong* vols 1 and 2, the DPM-MTR cassettes (*Seleksi Gambang Kromong Selendang Sutra Emas*, *Gambang Kromong Stambul Bunga Mawar*, *Gambang Kromong Cinta Damai*), *Gambang Kromong Tempo Dulu*, *Instrumental Gambang Kromong Betawi*, Tukang Tabuh's *Gambang Kromong Instrumental* EPs, and the orchestras whose names include it (Irama Bersatu, Slendang Betawi, Setia Muda, Sinar Indah Kebalen Jaya, Sinar Kramat, "Orchestra Gambang Keromong").
- **A page says so.** Indonesian Wikipedia calls Pang Tjin Nio (Masnah) the last maestro of classical gambang kromong and names the 1991 Folkways album as hers. The infobox of Benyamin Sueb gives his genre as Gambang Kromong and his former group as Naga Mustika. Go Jin leads GK Selendang Sutra Emas and Phang Tong Wie leads GK Sinar Kramat.
- **Ida Royani** is tied only through Naga Mustika recruiting her to sing with Benyamin, and through the album title.

The definition rests on English and Indonesian Wikipedia. The English article itself rests on one source, the Jakarta Encyclopedia.

**Weak ties and gaps:**
- Every Benyamin/Ida Royani, Sinar Kramat and Jetty track is from a compilation or reissue, so these tracks have no year. The playlist therefore has eight songs with a null year.
- The 1990s DPM-MTR cassettes credit singers or leaders ("Ating Uci Dkk", "Sony Ar & H. Toty"), not the orchestra.
- "Gambang Kombinasi" by Setia Muda stands for gambang kromong kombinasi only by its title.
- The size is a judgement, not a count.

## (c) Neighbours with no id in the tree
- **Tanjidor.** This is the Betawi brass band, and the closest neighbour. The Folkways album ends with a tanjidor piece: Grup Tanjidor Kembang Ros, "Cinta Manis".
- **Gambang kromong kombinasi and Betawi pop.** Gambang kromong mixed with Western band instruments, as in Benyamin S. and later koplo versions such as the *Gambang Kromong Koplo* compilation on iTunes. It is treated here as part of this genre, not a separate id.
- **Lenong and cokek.** Lenong is a theatre form and cokek a dance; both are accompanied by gambang kromong. Neither is a separate musical genre.

## (d) What fell short
- The original release years of Benyamin S. and Ida Royani's recordings were not found on a readable page. Discogs and the 1970s Remaco and Indah labels were not tried.
- None of Pang Tjin Nio's 1980s Irama Masa cassettes was found in a catalogue.
- No pre-war 78 rpm gambang kromong records were found, even though English Wikipedia says the genre peaked around 1937.
- The Smithsonian Folkways liner notes, which would give recording dates and instruments per track, were not read, because the site refuses scripts.
- Only one MusicBrainz search worked. The web service was busy on most other tries.
