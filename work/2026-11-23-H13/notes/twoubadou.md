# twoubadou: notes

## (a) Confidence

**`twoubadou`.** Fairly sure of the definition and of the core performers; less sure of the years and the size. All 70 claims are `found`. Every song was read from a raw iTunes lookup. The definition comes from English Wikipedia's Twoubadou article, with support from Music of Haiti and French Wikipedia. Each song is tied to the genre in one of three ways:
- **A page calls the performer twoubadou.** French Wikipedia covers Trio Select and Coupé Cloué ("Ils jouent leur propre version de twoubadou") and calls Ti Paris a "troubadour haïtien". English Wikipedia covers Coupé Cloué, Mizik Mizik's "Blakawout" and Haitiando.
- **A paragraph names the hit songs.** English Wikipedia says compas musicians brought twoubadou into Pa Manyen Fanm Nan, Ma Rose, Ou Pati and Tu Me Touches, and the Haiti Twoubadou tracks carry those titles.
- **The title only.** This covers Lole-Lolay (*Klasik Twoubadou*), Gifrants (*Twoubadou Sèk*), New York Twoubadou (*Vol. 2*) and the other Haiti Twoubadou tracks (the artist name).

**Weak ties and gaps:**
- "Map di" is Coupé Cloué's larger Ensemble Sélect at the compas edge. French Wikipedia lists twoubadou among his genres, but the tie is to him, not to that album.
- Many years are null because the albums read are reissues.
- The 1920 start in the tree is not on any page, which only says "early 20th century".
- Port-au-Prince as the origin is supported only by Trio Select playing its clubs.

The size is a judgement made before collecting, not a count.

## (c) Neighbours with no id in the tree
- **Cuban guajira / música campesina.** Wikipedia names it as twoubadou's Cuban source, but it has no id in the tree. I used `son` for the near-miss instead, because the parentage line already makes son the parent.
- **Haitian mini-jazz.** It is named on Music of Haiti, has no id and is not stretched to fit. The neighbouring ids that do exist are `meringue`, `kompa` and `mizikrasin`.
- **Tumba francesa.** This is Haitian-derived drumming in Cuba. It has no id, but it is not a near neighbour in sound.

## (d) What fell short
- **No Kandjo recording.** Kandjo (Auguste de Pradines) is the founding figure, but none of his recordings was found on a readable page.
- **Recalled performers left out.** Toto Nécessité, Ti Coca / Wanga Nègès and Althiery Dorival were recalled as twoubadou performers, but no page read ties them to the genre.
- **MusicBrainz was busy.** Its first two searches returned a busy error, so it was not used. Every song rests on iTunes alone, with one source per song.
- **Years.** Ti Paris and several Haiti Twoubadou years are unknown, because only reissue or compilation dates were read. Two album years conflict between iTunes and French Wikipedia (Trio Select), and Mizik Mizik's Blakawout is 1999 on Wikipedia and 2000 on iTunes.
- **The Haiti Direct compilation (Soundway).** Its title names twoubadou but does not say which tracks are twoubadou, so it was not used.
- **No pre-1970 twoubadou recording.** The playlist has none, although Trio Select's first album is dated 1960.
