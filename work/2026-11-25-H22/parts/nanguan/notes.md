# nanguan: notes (H22)

## (a) Confidence
Fairly sure of the record and the playlist. The definition rests on the Chinese Wikipedia article 南管 (long, well referenced) and UNESCO's nanyin page, with the English article used only where it agrees. Every song has a found claim; ties are strong for Tsai Hsiao-yueh (called a nanguan singer on her Chinese Wikipedia page, album title Nan-kouan), Wang Xinxin (named as a nanguan musician on the 南管 page; album titles name 南管), Li Baiyan (listed as a national nanyin inheritor) and 青和南管 and 台南南聲社 (names or album titles). Ties by album title only: 謝曉雪 and 周成在 on the 福建南音 anthology. Weakest parts: the begin year (1600 is an estimate), the peak windows (judgement), and the parent (yayue is the nearest existing id, not a stated parent). The playlist leans on Taiwan (Tsai, Nansheng She, Wang Xinxin, Qinghe); only three mainland Quanzhou singers are present.

## (c) Disagreements with our records
- None with data/ directly. English Wikipedia's history section writes "Quanzhou(廣州)" (廣州 is Guangzhou); our origin uses Quanzhou, as the Chinese article and UNESCO do.
- The suggestions file calls nanguan "one of the oldest living Chinese ensemble traditions"; the Chinese article supports that (同列為現存最古老的樂種), but the first dated record is Ming, so `began` is 1600, not a Tang or Song date.
- Our yayue record is Zhou ritual music (bells and chimes); nanguan's claimed ancestry is Han xianghe ge, qingshang yue and Tang daqu, which yayue only loosely covers.

## (d) Neighbours with no id
- Beiguan (北管), the northern-style Taiwanese counterpart (contrast stated in the Chinese article).
- Liyuan opera (梨園戲), Gaojia opera (高甲戲) and Quanzhou marionette theatre (提線木偶), which use nanguan tunes (UNESCO: "has influenced opera, puppet theatre"). Nanguan xi (南管戲) is a registered Taiwanese heritage item.
- Han and Tang ancestors (相和歌, 清商樂, 唐大曲).
- Bayin (八音) ensembles of the Hokkien world.

## (e) What fell short
- Han Tang Yuefu and Gang-a-Tsui, named in the task: no streaming or MusicBrainz release found, and their Wikipedia pages could not be read (rate limit; 漢唐樂府 not found under that title). They are absent from the playlist.
- MusicBrainz has Chinese titles for none of the Ocora tracks; Chinese titles come from the Chinese Wikipedia discography of 蔡小月.
- Mainland China: the iTunes China store returned nothing for 南音 / 泉州南音; only the 2001 anthology (Taiwan store) and an unnamed-performer 2026 album were found. Quanzhou Nanyin Troupe (泉州南音樂團) and Xiamen Nanyue Troupe recordings are missing.
- No Singapore (Siong Leng / 湘靈音樂社) or Philippine recordings found.
- Claim count (96) exceeds the ~60 budget, mostly because each Ocora song carries two claims (MusicBrainz track + Chinese title).
